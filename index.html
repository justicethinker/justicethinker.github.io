About

I read physics at university, spent four years on the quantum-to-classical boundary, and have been writing software for longer than that. Between the two I argue — competitively, in finals, and on behalf of students I coach.

The through-line is measurement: a model of a system that cannot be seen, a service that has to survive contact with real users, an argument tested against someone trying to break it. The method transfers, which is the only interesting part.
