Now building · Founder

Atelity: an AI memory for the relationships your work depends on.

Built first for real estate agents and recruiters: people whose work runs on remembering people.

  1. Meet someone

    A client, a candidate, a contact at an event.

  2. Record 30 seconds

    Say what you learned in a short voice note.

  3. Atelity remembers

    It keeps the details, reminds you when it matters, and drafts the follow-up.

It started when I lost every audio recording on my phone. I built notetoself_app so that could never happen again. Atelity grew out of that.
