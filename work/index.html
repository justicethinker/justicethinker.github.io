Now building

Atelity

An AI memory for the relationships your work depends on.

Built first for real estate agents and recruiters: people whose work runs on remembering people. You record a 30-second voice note after meeting someone. Atelity remembers the details, reminds you when it matters and drafts the follow-up.

It started when I lost every audio recording on my phone. I built notetoself_app so that could never happen again. Atelity grew out of that.
