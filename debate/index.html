How I argue

Four rules.

The standard I hold an argument to, whether I am making it or judging it.

  1. Rule 01

    Precision

    Say it short, say it once.

  2. Rule 02

    Evidence

    Claims must survive scrutiny.

  3. Rule 03

    Empathy

    State the other side in its strongest form before you answer it.

  4. Rule 04

    Impact

    Meant to change a position.
