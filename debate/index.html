Four rules

Non-negotiable. Applied to papers and code reviews as much as to debates.

01

Precision

Every word carries weight. Say it short, say it once.

02

Evidence

Claims have to survive scrutiny — sourced and reasoned, not asserted.

03

Empathy

State the other side in its strongest form before you answer it.

04

Impact

Arranged to change a position, not to fill an hour.
