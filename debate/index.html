Four rules

I use these for papers and code reviews too.

01

Precision

Say it short, say it once.

02

Evidence

Claims have to survive scrutiny — sourced and reasoned, not asserted.

03

Empathy

State the other side in its strongest form before you answer it.

04

Impact

Meant to change a position.
