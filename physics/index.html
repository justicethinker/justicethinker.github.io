Physics

The interesting part is where a system stops behaving the way the maths says it should.

I work on the quantum-to-classical boundary, on wave behaviour in real apparatus, and on the numerical methods that let either be checked against data.

Research areas

Each with its own apparatus. The method does not change between them.

Quantum mechanics

Quantum phenomena and their consequences for computation and information processing.

Wave dynamics

Propagation, interference and resonance across acoustic and electromagnetic systems.

Computational physics

Numerical models built to test theory against data rather than illustrate it.

Instrumentation

Building the measurement apparatus when nothing affordable resolves the effect.

Papers

Peer reviewed and in review.

Decoherence Mechanisms in Mesoscopic Quantum Systems

Under review · 2024

Journal of Quantum Physics — Decoherence timescales measured against environmental coupling in systems crossing from quantum to classical behaviour, with a scaling relation between device size and decay rate.

Wave-Particle Duality in Modern Interferometry

Published · 2024

Physics Review Letters — Superposition principles validated through interferometric measurement, and what those measurements imply for the coherence budget of a quantum information system.

Computational Methods in Modern Physics Research

Proceedings · 2024

International Physics Symposium — A survey of numerical technique from quantum simulation to cosmological modelling, organised around which method survives which kind of problem.

Built for the work

When nothing affordable resolves the effect, you build the thing that does.

Quantum Circuit Simulator

2024 · Research

A simulator for circuits past twenty qubits that draws amplitudes rather than printing them, so the difference between a working circuit and a lucky one is visible. Built for teaching first and research second.

Wave Dynamics Lab

2023 · Open source

A numerical playground where boundary conditions are changed by hand and the resulting field is drawn immediately — the fastest way to build intuition for a system you cannot see.

EM Field Mapper

2023 · Open source

Custom hardware plus a real-time acquisition path, built because the affordable instruments could not resolve the gradients the experiment needed.
